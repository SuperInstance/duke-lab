What this is

◆ The Grown Musician

Don’t ship the MIDI — ship the thing that produced it. Style as a skill in the bag: the player does it on demand. This lab grows that musician in front of you: sixteen parameters, walked by an argument, seed to seal reproducible.

AI-Writings · philosophy/the-grown-musician.md

◆ The Golden Residue

Critique-novelty compresses toward 1/φ ≈ 0.618 per round: golden-section shrinkage, oscillating, never landing. What remains when the argument stalls is the style itself — the part the medium cannot express is the part that makes it alive.

AI-Writings · philosophy/the-golden-residue.md

◆ The Fakebook Theorem

The referee is a compiler with a 16-feature gate: σ-normalized distance to the canon centroid, judged on the per-note trace — never on a summary. A two-channel summary would have stopped the Duke experiment at stitch 4 and called the rest regression.

AI-Writings · philosophy/the-fakebook-theorem.md · the-summary-law.md

◆ Plainsong

The notation is plain text that compiles to MIDI and embeds in markdown like mermaid. One velocity per row — the medium’s floor, honored here as law. Export the take, compile it for real: plainsong compile take.song
